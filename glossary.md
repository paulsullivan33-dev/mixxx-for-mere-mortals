# Glossary

**Analysis** — Mixxx's one-time
listening pass over a track:
waveform, beat grid, key and
loudness, stored in the database.

**Auto DJ** — The mode that plays
the Auto DJ queue by itself, with
automatic transitions.

**Beat grid** — The map analysis
lays over a track: its BPM and
where the first beat falls.

**Beatmatching** — Aligning two
tracks' tempos and beat positions
so they can play together.

**BPM** — Beats per minute; a
track's tempo.

**Broadcast profile** — The saved
description of a streaming server:
host, port, mountpoint, password,
format, bitrate.

**Channel strip** — One deck's
mixer controls: gain, EQ, filter,
fader.

**Crate** — A hand-filed folder of
tracks inside the library;
references, not copies.

**Crossfader** — The horizontal
slider choosing how much of each
side of the mixer the audience
hears.

**Cue point** — The marked position
the CUE button returns to.

**Deck** — One player: a track, its
waveform, transport and tempo
controls.

**EQ** — Equalisation; here, the
high/mid/low knobs on each channel.

**Gain** — A channel's input level
trim; also, the loudness correction
analysis computes (ReplayGain).

**History** — The dated playlist
Mixxx writes automatically for
every session.

**Hotcue** — A stored position in a
track that a button press jumps to
and plays from.

**Key** — A track's musical key, as
detected by analysis; a guide to
harmonic compatibility.

**Key lock** — Holding a track's key
steady while its tempo changes.

**Mapping** — The file (XML, often
with JavaScript) connecting a
hardware controller's controls to
Mixxx's.

**Mountpoint** — The path on an
Icecast server at which a stream
lives; part of its address.

**Quantize** — Snapping actions —
a play press, a hotcue — to the
nearest beat of the grid.

**ReplayGain** — A loudness
measurement stored per track, used
to level playback volumes.

**Rescan** — Telling Mixxx to walk
its music folders again for new or
removed files (Ctrl+Shift+L).

**Spinny** — The deck's spinning
disc; a picture of the record that
can be grabbed to nudge or scratch.

**Sync** — The button (and mode)
that matches a deck's tempo — and,
on play, its beat phase — to
another deck, using the grids.

**Timecode vinyl** — A record
carrying a timing signal instead of
music, letting a turntable control
a digital track.

**Waveform** — The drawn shape of a
track's sound: overview of the whole
song, plus a scrolling close-up.
